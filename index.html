Compare Plans Based on Your Client’s Expected
Medical Needs and Estimated Annual Costs

CompareMedicareNow helps insurance agents evaluate and compare 2027 Medicare Advantage plans in Maricopa County using a client’s expected medical utilization. Enter information about primary care visits, specialist visits, hospital stays, outpatient procedures, prescription drugs, dental and hearing benefits. The software uses available plan information to calculate estimated annual costs, including premiums, copays, deductibles, maximum out-of-pocket amounts and Part B Giveback benefits. Results distinguish verified figures, assumptions and incomplete categories so agents can understand the basis of each comparison.
