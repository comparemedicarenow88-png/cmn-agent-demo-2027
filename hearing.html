1Select Hearing Aid Need

Obtain the client's total net hearing aid price for each plan being compared. A client- or agent-supplied price overrides any existing copay estimate for that plan.

2Enter Client or Agent Hearing Prices

Enter the client’s total net cost for all hearing aids for each plan you want to compare. When no price is supplied, CMN uses a published per-aid copay estimate where available; plans without either figure remain unranked. Copay estimates require verification.

Hearing aid benefit categories, provider networks, annual limits and deductibles still require confirmation.
